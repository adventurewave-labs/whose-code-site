What the numbers actually say

Ancuria shows what a confession looks like. 37 commits are classified assisted from direct Co-Authored-By: Claude trailers the agent left behind, and the velocity signal flagged 152 commits on days that broke the author's own rhythm. Those are honest fingerprints, counted, not hidden.

Turbo-rig shows the hole the industry is standing over. 18,282 commits and effectively no machine evidence at all, because its history was written before agent tools started signing their work. The scanner says unknown on all of it, and unknown is the truthful answer: a repository without trail artifacts cannot prove anything about who wrote it or who approved it. That is not a defect in the scanner. That is the multi-billion-dollar problem the whole category is about to collide with.

The zero, stated precisely. No commit was classified assisted or machine at deciding confidence unless it carried a direct confession — on our repos, under this method. That is what a zero here can prove: indirect signals never decided a class. It is not a general accuracy guarantee, and the report itself says so.

The method

Version 0.2, stamped on every report: commit trailers naming known AI identities, bot accounts, review artifacts, and velocity outliers. Classes are assisted / machine / unknown. There is no "human" class by design: a commit message can prove machine involvement, never human authorship. Every record is cross-checked against git's own authoritative history, and anything the scanner cannot read fails the scan instead of guessing.

The scan itself was gated the same way we gate everything: an independent reviewer, 14 rounds on the scanner, 12 on the validation receipt, every finding fixed with a regression test before a human merged.

What this is for

Gartner says 70% of enterprises will abandon agentic AI built by vendor forward-deployed engineering by 2028 (press release). The reason nobody puts in the forecast: buyers may not own what they paid for, and they cannot prove the human part either. The answer is evidence, produced while the work happens. We built the scanner to audit it, and we built the trail layer to produce it. We ran both on ourselves before showing either to anyone.

whose-code is in private beta. The case study is public; the tool is available to pilot teams, diligence firms, and agencies who need to answer the ownership question before a buyer asks it. Pilot invitations go out through the adventurewave-labs GitHub org.

FAQ

What is whose-code?

An authorship scanner for agent-built code. It classifies commits as assisted, machine, or unknown using direct confessions and trail artifacts — never guessed authorship. It is in private beta; pilot invitations go out through the adventurewave-labs GitHub org.

What does the zero mean?

No commit was classified assisted or machine at deciding confidence without a direct confession — on these repos, under this method. It proves indirect signals never decided a class. It is not a general accuracy guarantee.

Where do the numbers come from?

The estate validation run (method v0.2) against three production repositories — turbo-rig, ancuria, and adventure-wave-explorer. The receipt is committed at docs/ESTATE-VALIDATION.md in the private scanner repo.
